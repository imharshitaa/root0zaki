I'm Harshitaa Ashish — a security research engineer at LEVO.AI, working on product security testing, API security research, and AI attack surface analysis. My background sits at the overlap between offensive security and engineering: I test systems, write exploit code, and build tooling that makes security testing more reproducible and systematic.

I hold a Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) certification from EC-Council and a BTech in Computer Science Engineering with a specialization in Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics from Vellore Institute of Technology (2021–2025, 8.01 GPA). I also completed IBM's AI Engineering Professional Certificate in early 2023.

What draws me to security is the engineering depth it requires. Understanding why authentication protocols break, how AI systems can be manipulated through their interfaces, and how to automate the repetitive parts of security testing so the interesting parts get the attention they deserve.
