/ about

I'm Harshitaa Ashish, a security research engineer at LEVO.AI, working on product security testing, API security research, and AI attack surface analysis. My work sits at the overlap between offensive security and engineering: testing systems, writing exploit code, and building tooling that makes security testing more reproducible and systematic.

I hold a BTech in Computer Science Engineering, specializing in Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics, from Vellore Institute of Technology (2021–2025, GPA 8.01). I'm also a Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) through EC-Council and completed IBM's AI Engineering Professional Certificate in February 2023.

What draws me to security is the engineering depth it requires: understanding why authentication protocols break, how AI systems can be manipulated through their interfaces, and how to automate repetitive security testing so the interesting problems get the attention they deserve.
